# Settings, reorganised (November 17, 2026)

Jake: "I can't find it [the phone microphone setting]. The settings menu looks like a disorganized 2-year-old came up
with it, it's an absolute mess. Some of it's way too long, some of the windows are not modular. Please reorganize and
fix the whole thing." Then: "It needs to be much simpler, cleaner, but also have simple and advanced settings in some
parts, like in More settings. Like why can I only record up to 1080p? I should be able to go as high or as low as I
want." And: "I should be able to have my settings open as a separate window that can be large or shrink or whatever."
And: "Phones should say remotes — the one that lets you use your phone or tablet as a remote."

## What was wrong

- Eleven tabs in one row, in no order a volunteer would guess (Look, Themes, Language, Songs, Auto-follow, Bible,
  Video, Media, Backups, Help, About), two of them with sub-pages of their own behind a switch.
- Media was one page of about 63 rows: new pictures and videos, the room's sound, recording, and every livestream
  feature added by five agents (destinations, cameras, sound, filters, scenes, cards, NDI, captions, the website
  player, the remote guest). The phone microphone row only appeared after a phone had already been chosen as a
  microphone, in the middle of that page: nobody could find it.
- Video held the screens, the stage layout and phone drawing; Network, More settings and saved setups were separate
  windows; the phone camera help and the phone microphone help each explained the "not private" warning.
- Every row's help text was always shown, so pages were walls of grey words.
- The picture size was 720p or 1080p only.
- Settings was an overlay inside the operator window, so it could never be bigger than it, and it covered the service.

advanced settings (the switch at the bottom of the sidebar, remembered on the computer). Pages that are one list

## The new Settings

### A window of its own

`SettingsWindow` (`ui/dialogs/settings.slint`, `src/ui/dialogs/settings_window.rs`) is a real window beside the
operator: any size from 640 × 440 to full screen, the sidebar a fixed 236 px, the page filling the rest and scrolling.
It is not modal: the operator keeps working while it is open. It remembers its size, place and monitor, the last page
and Show advanced settings in `settings-window.json` in the data folder (per computer, not in a backup); when its
monitor is gone it opens on the main monitor, kept wholly on it. Slint keeps each window's globals apart, so all the
Settings code reaches its `SettingsDlg` (and `ThemesDlg`, `KeysDlg`, `SectionsDlg`, `NetworkDlg`, `StreamMoreDlg`)
through `settings_window::win(op)`.

Save and Cancel stay at the bottom right ("Cancel", "Save settings"; "Save theme" while a theme is edited). Save
saves and closes. Cancel, Esc or the window's close button close at once when nothing changed, else ask "Save your
changes to the settings?" (Save / Don't save / Cancel). Questions Settings asks show over the Settings window, not
over the operator behind it (`ask::in_settings`, or whichever window has the keyboard).

A category with one page is one line. The page's title says where it is ("Livestream → Sound"), the same path every
"Settings → …" text in the app uses.

### Rows

Every setting is a `SetRow` (`ui/dialogs/settings-row.slint`): its name, "Advanced" in faint letters when it is one, a
small (?) that shows its help (help is never shown until asked for), then its controls. Same padding and spacing on
every page. A setting that another page needs is mentioned there as a link button ("A phone as a microphone: Settings
→ Remote and network") that jumps to the row and lights it up.

**The phone microphone** is now on Remote and network → Phone camera and microphone: the address to open on the phone,
the one-time "not private" step (said once for the camera and the microphone), whether the stage screen on phones is
on (the phone's page needs it), and a link to Livestream → Sound, where "Phone" is chosen in the Microphone or line-in
list. That list's help and its own link point back. Searching "phone mic", "phone microphone", "microphone", "lapel"
or "wireless" finds both rows.

### Find a setting

`settings_find.rs` holds every row: its key, page, whether it is advanced, its label and help (the same texts as the
Slint row; a test checks), and other words people use ("phone mic", "chords", "NDI", "key", "resolution", "4K",
"bitrate"…, translated like any text). Typing shows the rows and pages that have every word typed, the ones whose
name has them first, each with its path. Enter or a click opens the page, turns on Show advanced settings if the row
needs it, scrolls to the row and lights it up for 2.5 seconds.

### Any picture size

`record::Preset` is now 480p, 720p, 1080p, 1440p, 4K or a size typed by hand (Custom, 320 × 180 to 7680 × 4320, made
even). Saved as before ("720p", "1080p") or "1600x900"; anything unreadable loads as 1080p, so an old or odd settings
file always loads. The bitrate Automatic uses grows with the size (1500, 3000, 6000, 9000, 20000 kbit/s; a typed size
from its pixels) and by half again above 30 pictures a second; the bitrate list goes from 1500 to 50000 kbit/s.
Settings warns, but allows, a size that is likely more than this computer can make (by its processor count), past
4096 wide for H.264 (graphics card encoders stop there), or that needs a lot of upload. The upload speed test keeps
the size chosen when it fits and suggests the biggest smaller one that does. A destination that takes less than the
size chosen (Facebook, Twitch, Instagram, X and TikTok: 1080p; YouTube: 4K) is named on Where to stream and gets a
smaller copy made while live (`Quality::for_service`), not a failed stream.

### Remote

The phone or tablet that runs the slides is the **remote** everywhere (glossary updated, "phone remote" retired in
`tests/words.rs`): "Remote", "Remote password", "Pair a phone or tablet…", "Use your phone or tablet as a remote".
The phone camera and phone microphone keep "phone".

## Settings paths

Every "Settings → …" text was changed to the new places ("Settings → Livestream → Sound", "Settings → Remote and
network", "Settings → Auto-follow"…); `tests/words.rs` retires the old ones ("Settings → Media", "Settings → Video",
"Settings → Network…", "Settings → Help").

## Tests

- `settings_find`: searching "phone microphone", "phone mic", "4K", "NDI", "chords", "key" lands on the right rows.
- Every page can be shown; on each, with Show advanced settings on, the rows drawn are exactly the rows `settings_find`
  lists for it (same label and help), so a row on no page or on two fails; advanced rows are hidden when it is off.
- The window remembers its page, Show advanced settings, size and place; it comes back on the main monitor.
- The Settings window at 1024 × 768 and at 640 × 440: its Save and Cancel are inside it on every page.
- Saving still writes the same `Settings`/`RecordPrefs` fields (the existing form tests, moved to the new pages).
- Picture sizes: old settings load, sizes reach FFmpeg's arguments, bitrates scale, capped destinations get a copy.

## Not done

- Screens, Stage layouts, Your scenes and the song editor are still overlays in the operator window, not windows of
  their own: each has its own state tied to the operator's dialog layer (`DialogKind`, `dialog_refresh`, the
  operator's asks), and the dock-panels work is rebuilding that window at the same time. They fit from 1280 × 720 up
  as before. Settings shows the approach (`settings_window.rs`) for doing them next.
