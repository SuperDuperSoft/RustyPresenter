# Your first Sunday

This takes you from a fresh install to a service you can run. Allow about twenty minutes, on a day that is not Sunday.

The first time RustyPresenter opens, it offers three short steps that cover the start of this page. You can skip them; **Help → Getting started…** shows them again.

## 1. Connect the screens

1. Plug the projector or TV into the computer.
2. Press `Win + P` and choose **Extend**. (If you choose Duplicate, RustyPresenter cannot tell the screens apart.)
3. In RustyPresenter, open **Window → Screens…**
4. Pick which monitor is the **audience screen**. **Identify** shows a label on each monitor so you can tell which is which.
5. If you have a screen facing the band, pick it as the **stage screen**.

If the list shows only one screen, check the cable, press `Win + P` again, and click **Refresh**.

## 2. Make the service

1. **File → New service…** and give it a name, like the date.
2. **File → Add to service** and choose what to add:
   - **Song…** to pick a song from your library or type a new one.
   - **Bible passage…** for a reading.
   - **Video & media…** for a video, a picture or music.
   - **Slides…** for a PowerPoint, Keynote or PDF file.
   - **Announcement…** for a simple text slide.
3. Drag the items in the service list to put them in order.

You can also drag files from a folder straight onto the window.

## 3. Check it

**Help → Check the service** looks for missing files and empty items. Fix what it finds.

Then click through every item once. Click a slide and look at the audience screen.

## 4. Run it

- **Click a slide** to show it. The red outline marks what the room sees.
- `→` or `Space` goes to the next slide, and keeps going into the next item. `←` goes back.
- `F1` clears everything. `F2` clears only the words and leaves the background.
- `F5` shows your church logo.

Next week, **File → Services…** opens an older service or copies last week's as a start.
