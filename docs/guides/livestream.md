# Livestream and recording

## Record the service

Click **Record** at the top when the service starts, and click it again at the end. **File → Recording settings…** has the choices for the recording.

## Livestream

Set this up once, on a weekday.

1. Open **Settings → Settings…** and go to **Livestream**.
2. Switch a **destination** on, such as YouTube or Facebook.
3. Either **sign in**, or paste the **stream key** from that service.
4. Choose the picture and the sound for the stream.

A **Go Live** button now appears beside Record.

On Sunday: press **Go Live** when the service starts, and press it again at the end.

### About stream keys

A stream key is a password. Anyone who has it can stream as you. RustyPresenter keeps it in this computer's own password store, never in a backup or a service file. Do not put it in an email or a chat.

### Sound

The stream needs its own sound: a microphone, or a line from your sound board into the computer. A line from the sound board usually sounds much better than a microphone in the room.

Test with a private or unlisted stream before your first Sunday, and listen to it on a phone with headphones.

## Cameras

A phone on the church Wi-Fi can be a camera or a microphone for the stream. Set it up in **Settings → Remote and network…**
