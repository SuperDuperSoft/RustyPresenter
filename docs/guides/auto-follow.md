# Auto-follow

Auto-follow listens to the singing and moves the song slides for you.

> This is the newest part of RustyPresenter. It is good at following a song sung in its usual order. When the band does something unexpected it can be a couple of seconds late. Keep a hand near the keyboard.

## What you need

- **Sound from the singing** coming into the computer. The best is the lead vocal sent from the sound board. A microphone near the singers also works.
- **The speech model**, a file RustyPresenter downloads once, from the Auto-follow pages of Settings.

## Turn it on

1. Open **Settings → Settings…** and go to **Auto-follow**.
2. Choose the microphone or line-in that carries the singing.
3. Download the speech model on the **Speech model** page.
4. Put a song on the screen. Auto-follow shows there while the song is live.

You are always in charge. Pressing `→`, `←` or clicking a slide moves at once.

## Teach it your band

Bands rarely sing a song exactly as written. Auto-follow can learn how yours does it.

1. At rehearsal, click **Record a practice** and run the slides by hand as you would on Sunday.
2. Afterwards click **Learn from the last recording**.

It learns the order each song was really sung in (the chorus after every verse, the bridge twice) and how long each slide was up. On Sunday it expects that order.

**Check the last recording** plays the practice back through auto-follow and compares it with your slide changes. Nothing changes on the screens.

## If it gets it wrong

Press the key for where you want to be.
