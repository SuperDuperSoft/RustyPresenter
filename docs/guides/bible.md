# Bible

## Show a passage

1. Press `Ctrl + B`. The cursor goes to the Bible box at the top.
2. Type a reference, like `John 3:16-18` or `Psalm 23`.
3. Press `Enter` to add it to the service. Press `Enter` again to show it.

Don't know the reference? **Edit → Find a verse…** finds a passage by its words.

## Which Bibles you get

| Bible | Needs internet | Needs anything else |
| --- | --- | --- |
| KJV, WEB, BSB | No | Nothing. They are built in. |
| NIV | Yes | Nothing. |
| ESV | Yes | A free key from Crossway. **Settings → Bible…** shows where to get it. |
| Over a thousand more, in over a thousand languages | Some | Add them in **Settings → Bible…** |

## Add more Bibles

1. Open **Settings → Bible…**
2. Search by name or by language, for example `Spanish` or `Reina`.
3. Click **Add**.

Each Bible has a tag:

- **Offline** Bibles are downloaded once and then work with no internet.
- **Online** Bibles (from YouVersion) are read as you look each passage up, so the computer needs internet. A passage you have already looked up is remembered for a while.

If Sunday's internet is not reliable, prefer an Offline Bible, or look your passages up before the service while you are connected.

Bibles written right to left, such as Hebrew and Arabic, are not offered yet.

## Two or three Bibles side by side

This is useful for a service in more than one language.

1. In the Bible box, click the **Side by side** chip.
2. Pick up to two more Bibles. That makes three with your main one.
3. Choose how they share the slide.

Every new passage then shows in all of them. Open the same place again to turn them off.

## Credits

Some publishers ask for a credit line on the slide. RustyPresenter adds it by itself where it is needed. **Settings → Bible…** shows each Bible's notice.

## Bibles that are not available

NLT, CSB and HCSB are not offered at the moment, because their publishers have not licensed them for the app. If your church has its own API.Bible key that covers one of them, add the key in **Settings → Bible…** and it appears.
