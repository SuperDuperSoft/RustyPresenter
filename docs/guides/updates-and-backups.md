# Updates and backups

## New versions

RustyPresenter checks for a new version when it starts, at most once a day, and tells you when there is one. It does not ask while something is on the screen.

To update:

1. Close RustyPresenter.
2. Download the new `RustyPresenter-Setup-<version>.exe` from the [releases page](https://github.com/SuperDuperSoft/RustyPresenter/releases/latest).
3. Double-click it.

Your songs, services and settings stay as they are.

Do not update on Sunday morning. Update on a weekday and click through a service first.

To turn the check off, open **Help → About RustyPresenter** and switch off **Check for a new version when RustyPresenter starts**.

## Backups

A full backup (songs, services, settings, and every video, picture and slide) is made each time RustyPresenter starts and once a day. The last ten are kept.

To go back to one: **Settings → Backups…**, pick a backup, click **Restore**. A copy of what you had is kept first.

## Where your work is kept

In `%LOCALAPPDATA%\RustyPresenter` on this computer. (Paste that into the address bar of File Explorer to open it.) It is not in OneDrive.

## Moving to another computer

**File → Export → Service bundle…** saves a service with its songs and files in one file. Open it on the other computer with **File → Open a service file…**

## Removing RustyPresenter

**Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps → RustyPresenter → Uninstall.** Your songs and services are kept unless you tick **Also delete that folder**.
