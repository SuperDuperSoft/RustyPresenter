Invite a remote guest

A guest speaker or missionary joins from a browser at home. Their picture comes into the livestream as a camera, and their voice through this computer's sound (the room hears them too). Keep this page open while they are on.

1. Send the guest two things: the page to open, and this invitation code.

Save the guest's page

Attach that file to an email to the guest. They double-click it, paste the code, and allow their camera.

2. The guest sends back a reply code. Paste it here:

3. To put the guest on the stream: in RustyPresenter, Settings → Media → Livestream → Remote guest, choose "Use the guest as a camera", then switch to that camera while live.
