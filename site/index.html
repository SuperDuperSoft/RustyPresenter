What it does

Everything a Sunday needs, in one window.

Songs

Type or paste the words, or bring them in from SongSelect. Each verse and chorus gets its own label and colour.

Bible

KJV, WEB and BSB are built in and need no internet. NIV works online, and over a thousand more Bibles can be added. Up to three show side by side.

Slides, videos and music

PowerPoint, Keynote and PDF files, pictures, and almost any video or audio file.

Two screens

The audience screen for the room, and a stage screen for the band and the speaker with the words and messages from the operator.

Phone remote

Run the slides from a phone or tablet on the same Wi-Fi. Nothing to install on the phone.

Livestream and recording

Send the service to YouTube or Facebook, or save it as a video.

Auto-follow

The app listens to the singing and moves the song slides by itself.

14 languages

The app itself is translated, and anyone can fix or add a translation inside the app, with no programming.

Works without internet

Songs, the built-in Bibles, slides and videos need no internet during the service. Your songs and services stay on your own computer.

Your first service in five minutes

The app walks you through these the first time it opens. You can skip that and come back to it from the Help menu.

  1. Plug in the projector or TV and set the computer to extend the desktop onto it. Open Window → Screens… and pick the audience screen.
  2. Add things: a song, a Bible passage, a video or slides, from File → Add to service. Or drag files onto the window.
  3. Click a slide. It is on the screen at once. The red outline shows what the room sees.
  4. During the service, press → or Space for the next slide and ← to go back. F1 clears the screen.
